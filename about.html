About Aarav

Hey there! I'm Aarav, a photographer based in Ottawa with a passion for capturing authentic moments that tell your unique story.

For the past 5 years, I've been helping people in the Ottawa area feel comfortable in front of the camera. Whether it's a professional headshot, a lifestyle session in the city, or documenting your special event, I focus on creating a relaxed environment where your personality can shine through.

My approach is simple: natural light, genuine expressions, and photos you'll actually want to share. No awkward poses, no stress — just you, looking your best.

📍 Based in Ottawa, ON
